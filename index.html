S&P 500 Valuation Analysis

A regression study testing whether the Shiller PE Ratio (PE10) holds up as a predictor of long-run, inflation-adjusted market prices.

  • 1,768 monthly observations spanning 1871–2018
  • Validated normality and homoscedasticity assumptions via residual diagnostics
Finding — PE10 explains 58% of variance in real market prices (R² = 0.58, p < 2e-16)

Prion Protein Fibril-Membrane Data Analysis

A structural bioinformatics study on how prion protein fibrils interact with cell membranes, conducted through UC Merced's Undergraduate Research Opportunities Center (UROC).

  • Coarse-grained molecular dynamics simulations using the Martini forcefield and GROMACS
  • Measured fibril residue distances to the membrane surface and characterized residue exposure using SASA and hydropathy
  • Identified localized membrane deformation at the fibril anchoring site
